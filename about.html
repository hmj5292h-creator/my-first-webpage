How North Star Bakery Began

North Star Bakery started with a simple dream: to create a cozy place where everyone could enjoy freshly baked treats and feel at home. Our little bakery was inspired by the warmth of homemade baking and the playful personality of cats. From our first loaf of bread to our signature cat-shaped treats, we make everything with care and a little bit of fun. Today, North Star Bakery is a friendly neighborhood bakery where delicious food, warm smiles, and happy cats come together. 🐾


Our Sourcing Philosophy

At North Star Bakery, we believe that great baking starts with good ingredients. We carefully choose fresh, high-quality ingredients from trusted local suppliers whenever possible. We use fresh fruits, quality flour, and seasonal ingredients to make our breads, pastries, and cakes. Good ingredients + careful baking = purr-fect treats. 🐱🥐
